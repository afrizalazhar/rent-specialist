Redesign the landing page of a vehicle rental business into a modern, premium, professional, and conversion-focused website.

## BUSINESS CONTEXT

- The business provides vehicle rental services.
- There is NO online booking system.
- There is NO checkout, online payment, booking form, or reservation engine.
- All customer inquiries and reservations are handled directly through WhatsApp.
- The primary goal of the website is to build trust, showcase the available vehicles, explain the rental services, and drive customers to WhatsApp.
- The website should feel like a real, established vehicle rental business, not a generic SaaS template or vehicle marketplace.

---

# DESIGN DIRECTION

Use a:

- Clean
- Modern
- Premium
- Automotive-inspired
- Professional
- Conversion-focused

visual style.

Use generous whitespace, strong typography, clear visual hierarchy, and high-quality vehicle photography.

The design should feel sophisticated without becoming overly luxurious or corporate.

### Visual Principles

- Large, high-quality vehicle photography
- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Clean grid system
- Subtle rounded corners
- Minimal shadows
- Minimal gradients
- Avoid excessive glassmorphism
- Avoid overly decorative UI
- Avoid excessive animations
- Avoid generic SaaS-style layouts
- Avoid overly colorful interfaces

Use a dark charcoal / near-black visual foundation combined with off-white and light gray sections.

Use one strong accent color for important CTAs, especially WhatsApp-related actions.

The overall impression should be:

**"A trustworthy, professional vehicle rental company that is easy to contact."**

Not:

**"A generic landing page template with cars on it."**

---

# PAGE STRUCTURE

## 1. NAVIGATION BAR

Create a clean sticky navigation bar.

Navigation items:

- Fleet
- Services
- Why Us
- How It Works
- FAQ
- Contact

Right-side primary CTA:

**Chat on WhatsApp**

Keep the navbar minimal and compact.

On mobile, use a clean hamburger menu and keep the WhatsApp CTA highly accessible.

---

# 2. HERO SECTION

Create a strong, visually dominant hero section.

The hero should immediately communicate:

1. What the business offers
2. Why customers should trust it
3. What action they should take

Example headline:

**"Rent the Right Vehicle for Every Journey."**

Supporting text:

**"Well-maintained vehicles for business trips, holidays, daily transportation, and every journey in between."**

Primary CTA:

**Chat on WhatsApp**

Secondary CTA:

**Explore Our Fleet**

Use a large premium vehicle photograph as the primary visual.

The hero should NOT contain:

- Date pickers
- Location selectors
- Search availability
- Booking widgets
- Online reservation forms

Because this business does not offer online booking.

Add small trust indicators beneath the CTA:

- Well-Maintained Fleet
- Transparent Pricing
- Fast Response

---

# 3. TRUST BAR

Immediately below the hero, create a compact trust section.

Example:

**Well-Maintained Vehicles**  
**Clear Pricing**  
**Easy Rental Process**  
**WhatsApp Support**

Keep this section visually simple and credible.

Do not use exaggerated claims such as:

- #1 Rental Company
- Cheapest Prices
- Best Rental Service in Indonesia

unless verified data is provided.

# WHATSAPP CONVERSION SYSTEM

WhatsApp is the primary conversion mechanism throughout the entire website.

Use contextual CTA labels:

- Chat on WhatsApp
- Ask About Availability
- Check Availability
- Ask About This Vehicle
- Contact Us
- Get Rental Information

Every vehicle-specific WhatsApp CTA should ideally generate a contextual pre-filled message.

Example:

**"Hi, I'm interested in renting the Toyota Avanza. Is it available for my preferred dates?"**

The user should never feel like they are being pushed through a fake booking system.

---

# MOBILE EXPERIENCE

The website must be designed mobile-first.

On mobile:

- Keep vehicle images prominent.
- Keep typography readable.
- Maintain generous spacing.
- Make WhatsApp CTAs easy to reach.
- Add a persistent floating WhatsApp button.
- Keep navigation simple.
- Avoid oversized sections that require excessive scrolling.
- Make vehicle cards easy to scan.
- Keep important information visible without unnecessary interaction.

The mobile experience should feel like a polished commercial website, not a compressed desktop website.

---

# UX & ANIMATION

Use subtle interactions:

- Smooth scrolling
- Gentle hover effects
- Image scale on hover
- Subtle section reveal animations
- Button interaction states

Animations should support usability and visual polish.

Do NOT use:

- Excessive parallax
- Constant floating elements
- Aggressive animations
- Excessive glass effects
- Overly complicated transitions

The website should feel fast and professional.

---

# COPYWRITING STYLE

Use natural, concise, confident English.

The copy should be:

- Clear
- Human
- Professional
- Concise
- Customer-focused
- Trustworthy

Avoid:

- Corporate jargon
- Empty marketing claims
- Excessive adjectives
- Generic AI-sounding copy
- Fake urgency
- Unsupported claims

Focus on practical customer benefits.

---

# IMPORTANT FUNCTIONAL CONSTRAINT

This website is NOT an online rental marketplace.

There must be NO:

- Online booking system
- Checkout
- Payment gateway
- Booking calendar
- Availability search engine
- Customer account system
- Shopping cart
- Reservation dashboard

The website's conversion funnel is intentionally simple:

**Discover → Browse Fleet → Build Trust → Contact via WhatsApp**

Optimize the entire landing page around this funnel.

---

# FINAL DESIGN GOAL

The final website should look like a polished, premium vehicle rental company that could realistically operate in a competitive market.

It should communicate:

**Quality vehicles. Clear information. Easy communication. No unnecessary friction.**

Prioritize:

1. Trust
2. Vehicle presentation
3. Clear services
4. Strong visual hierarchy
5. WhatsApp conversion
6. Mobile usability
7. Professional appearance
